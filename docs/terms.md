# Terms

Carbon Arc licenses its data to each firm that buys it, under that firm's
own agreement with Carbon Arc. This plugin uses Carbon Arc's published API
with the key your firm enters.

- **Your own key, your own data.** Each deployment uses its own key, and
  nothing is shared between deployments.
- **Buying is yours.** Data is bought per framework, priced before purchase
  in Carbon Arc's own platform. This plugin never prices or buys.
- **Keeping bought data** is your firm's contract with Carbon Arc. The
  plugin keeps Carbon Arc's answers as its settings say, and enforces
  nothing: read your agreement before choosing how long to keep them.
- **Work product** shared outside your firm credits Carbon Arc, as its
  terms ask.
