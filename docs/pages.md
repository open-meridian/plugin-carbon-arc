# Pages

All three pages are under **Manage**, for the plugin's admins. None shows a
panel's values. Each is also a read tool on the deployment's MCP, answering
what the page shows.

## Connection (`read_connection`)

How the plugin reaches Carbon Arc: the mode (Live, Sample data or No key),
whether the key is set (never its value), the frameworks kept current,
whether answers are kept, Carbon Arc's limits and the rate the plugin keeps.

## Datasets (`read_datasets`)

What the plugin serves the lake: none in this version, and why. Any want
the lake hands it is listed as declined. Carbon Arc's terms in words.

## Frameworks (`read_purchased_frameworks`)

Each framework you named: held, not held (Carbon Arc has none for your key)
or failing, its insight, aggregation and entity-mapping version. Then each
entity they measure, by Carbon Arc's ID, with its ticker and your
deployment's instrument, or why none.
